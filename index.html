Our Story

Quanzhou Reallyou Import & Export Co., Ltd. is a China-based footwear sourcing and manufacturing team specializing in OEM & ODM development for global brands.

I believe every company has its history... So how was our company built?

Initially, our founder worked across every stage of footwear manufacturing and mastered every process. In her 30s, she had already become an expert in footwear production. She is a typical Chinese power woman — highly responsible, detail-oriented and extremely strict about quality.

Later, she brought together a group of professional friends from the footwear industry and founded Reallyou over six years ago. Since then, we have worked with many international brands. While doing our best to provide excellent service, we have continued to grow together with our customers.

Of course, we faced many challenges along the way, but we overcame every one of them and always delivered satisfying results for our clients.

So, please trust us. We are a responsible, efficient and sincere team that is ready to help your business grow.

HOW WE WORK

From Idea to Delivery

We connect buyers, materials, factories and production into one coordinated process — from the first discussion to final shipment.

01

Product Development & Design

We review the design, target customer, construction, expected price range and technical requirements before development begins.

02

Negotiation & Coordination

We communicate with customers and negotiate with suppliers on materials, pricing, construction, quantities, lead times and practical production solutions.

03

Material & Sampling

Materials, colors and components are sourced and evaluated before prototypes, fitting samples and development revisions are completed.

04

Pre-Production

Fit, materials, colors, workmanship and production specifications are confirmed before bulk manufacturing starts.

05

Production & QC

Our team follows production progress and checks critical quality points during manufacturing and before shipment.

06

Packing & Delivery

Final inspection, packing and shipment are coordinated according to your delivery schedule and destination requirements.
