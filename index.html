About Us

Quanzhou Reallyou Import & Export Co., Ltd. is a China-based footwear sourcing and manufacturing partner specializing in OEM & ODM development for global brands.

With deep roots in Jinjiang’s footwear industry cluster, we integrate design, sampling, production coordination, and quality control into a streamlined supply chain system. Our focus is not just manufacturing, but delivering scalable product solutions that align with brand positioning, cost structure, and market demand.

Over the years, we have built long-term partnerships with factories and material suppliers across multiple categories including sports, casual, outdoor, and sandals. This allows us to respond quickly to market trends, reduce sampling lead time, and maintain stable production quality across different order volumes.

We work closely with clients from initial concept development to final shipment, ensuring clear communication, consistent execution, and reliable delivery. Our goal is to become a long-term strategic sourcing partner rather than a one-time supplier.
