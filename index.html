Our Story

Quanzhou Reallyou Import & Export Co., Ltd. is a China-based footwear sourcing and manufacturing team specializing in OEM & ODM development for global brands.

I believe every company has its history... So how was our company built?

Initially, our founder worked across every stage of footwear manufacturing and mastered every process. In her 30s, she had already become an expert in footwear production. She is a typical Chinese power woman — highly responsible, detail-oriented and extremely strict about quality.

Later, she brought together a group of professional friends from the footwear industry and founded Reallyou over six years ago. Since then, we have worked with many international brands. While doing our best to provide excellent service, we have continued to grow together with our customers.

Of course, we faced many challenges along the way, but we overcame every one of them and always delivered satisfying results for our clients.

So, please trust us. We are a responsible, efficient, sincere team that is ready to help your business grow.
